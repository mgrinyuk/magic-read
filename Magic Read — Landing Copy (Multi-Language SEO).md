# Magic Read — Landing page copy (multi-language SEO)

**Strategy in one line:** one focused landing page per language, each ranking for "[language] pronunciation practice," with "any text you bring, scored word by word" as the differentiator that converts once people land. No "AI" — the mechanism is **speech recognition**, the proof is **word-by-word** scoring.

Order reflects search priority: English (largest market) → Chinese (deepest feature set) → Russian → the rest.

---

## Shared building blocks (reuse on every page)

**How it works**

1. **Paste any text** — an article, your homework, a dialogue, song lyrics, subtitles.
2. **Listen** — every sentence is read aloud in a natural voice.
3. **Tap any word** — instant translation and reading help, in context.
4. **Say it back** — read the sentence aloud; speech recognition scores every word.
5. **Drill what you miss** — low-scoring words are practiced one chunk at a time until they stick, then saved to flashcards.

**Differentiator line**

> Not a fixed course. Magic Read works with the real texts you actually want to read — so you practice the words you'll really use.

**Primary CTA**

> Paste your first text — free to start.

## SEO notes for the developer

- **One page per language** at the slugs above; link them from a footer language switcher. Each page targets a distinct "[language] pronunciation practice" query — a single page can't rank for eight.
- **hreflang / language targeting:** these pages are all in English (aimed at people who *search in English* for "[language] pronunciation practice"). If you later localize the UI copy itself, add `hreflang` tags so Google serves the right version.
- **Long-tail moat:** build short blog posts / FAQ entries around the phrases no competitor owns — "practice pronunciation with your own text," "read [language] text aloud with feedback," "import text pronunciation practice." Low competition, exact product match.
- **"AI" is intentionally absent.** Use "speech recognition" and "scored word by word" everywhere instead — more credible, no chatbot connotation, still solid search volume.
- **Differentiator does the converting:** keep "any text you bring" in every H1 — it's the one claim no course-based competitor (Du Chinese, HelloChinese, ELSA) can make.
