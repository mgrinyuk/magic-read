Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies such as localStorage to store preferences on your device.

2. Cookies we use

Strictly necessary

These are required for the service to function and cannot be disabled.

NamePurposeDuration

localStorage: selectedVoice_*Remembers your chosen TTS voice per languagePersistent

Functional

These improve your experience but are not essential.

NamePurposeDuration

localStorage: targetLangRemembers your last selected target languagePersistent

Analytics (optional)

We do not currently use third-party analytics cookies. If we introduce them in future we will update this policy and ask for your consent.

3. How to control cookies

You can clear cookies and localStorage at any time via your browser settings. Note that clearing session cookies will log you out. Most browsers also let you block third-party cookies.
