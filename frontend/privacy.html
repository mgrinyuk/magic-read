Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Who we are

[BUSINESS NAME], KVK [KVK NUMBER], operates the Magic Read service available at magicread.app. For privacy matters contact us at support@magicread.app.

2. What data we collect

We collect the following personal data:

  • Account data — email address and password hash when you create an account via Supabase Auth.
  • Content data — texts you paste or save, flashcard decks and cards you create, and language preferences.
  • Usage data — anonymous request logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) retained for up to 30 days.
  • Payment data — if you subscribe, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store card numbers; Stripe shares only subscription status with us.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide and improve the Magic Read service.
  • To authenticate your session and sync your saved texts and flashcards.
  • To generate audio via Google Cloud Text-to-Speech (text is sent to Google's API and not stored by us).
  • To translate words and sentences via Google Cloud Translation.
  • To send transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset).

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

We process your data on the basis of:

  • Contract performance — to deliver the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • Consent — for optional cookies and analytics (see our Cookie Policy).

5. Data sharing

We share data only with:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (EU servers available).
  • Google Cloud — Text-to-Speech and Translation APIs.
  • Stripe — payment processing (if applicable).
  • Render — backend hosting.
  • Vercel — frontend hosting.

We do not sell your data to third parties.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and content data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by emailing support@magicread.app. We will delete your data within 30 days.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@magicread.app to exercise any of these rights.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth and never stored in plain text. We apply industry-standard security practices but cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will notify you by email or in-app notice of material changes. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.
