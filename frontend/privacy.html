1. Introduction

Welcome to Magic Read.

Magic Read is a language learning platform that helps users practice reading, listening, pronunciation, vocabulary, and comprehension using real-world texts.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect your email address, name or nickname if provided, password authentication data handled by our authentication provider, user ID, and account preferences.

User Content

We may store texts uploaded or pasted by users, saved vocabulary, flashcards, pronunciation practice results, and reading progress.

Purchases

When you buy a subscription, we may store purchase status, plan type, transaction identifiers, subscription dates, and payment provider information. We do not store full payment card numbers.

Technical Information

We may automatically collect device type, browser type, operating system, IP address, language settings, app interactions, usage counters, diagnostics, and error logs.

Audio / Speech Data

When you choose to use pronunciation or speech recognition features, microphone audio may be processed temporarily through speech recognition providers to generate pronunciation feedback. We do not intentionally store raw voice recordings unless explicitly stated.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to provide the Magic Read service, process texts and generate learning content, improve pronunciation and reading features, authenticate users, save user progress, provide customer support, improve platform performance and security, and prevent abuse or fraud.

4. Third-Party Services

Magic Read may use third-party providers including Supabase (authentication and database), Stripe (payments and subscriptions), Google Play Billing (Android in-app purchases and subscriptions), Vercel (hosting), Render (backend hosting), Google Cloud services, and speech recognition providers. These services may process limited user data as required for functionality. We are not responsible for third-party privacy practices.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use third-party advertising SDKs in the Android app.

5. Payments

Payments are processed securely through Stripe or Google Play Billing, depending on where the purchase is made. Magic Read does not store full payment card information. Subscription billing, taxes, refunds, and payment processing are handled by the relevant payment provider and related financial providers.

6. Cookies

Magic Read may use cookies and similar technologies to keep users logged in, remember preferences, analyze traffic and usage, and improve functionality. Users may disable cookies through browser settings, though some features may stop functioning correctly. See our Cookie Policy for details.

7. Data Retention

We retain user data only as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and improve the platform. Users may delete their account in the app from Account → Delete account, or request deletion by emailing support@magicread.app from the email address connected to the account.

Account deletion removes the Magic Read account and associated learning data such as saved texts, vocabulary, flashcards, usage progress, and profile preferences, except where limited records must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, tax, or payment compliance reasons.

8. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access your data, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, object to certain processing, and request export of your data. Requests may be sent to support@magicread.app.

9. Children

Magic Read is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data, including encryption in transit for data sent between the app and our services. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their passwords.

11. International Users

By using Magic Read, users understand that information may be processed in multiple countries depending on infrastructure and service providers.

12. Google Play Data Safety Summary

For the Magic Read Android app, the main data types collected are account information, user-provided learning content, optional voice/audio for pronunciation features, purchase history, app activity, diagnostics, and device or other IDs used for app functionality, account management, analytics, security, and compliance.

Magic Read does not collect precise location, contacts, calendar, SMS, photos, videos, health data, or installed apps. Data collection for microphone-based pronunciation practice is optional and happens only when you use those features.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published on this page.
