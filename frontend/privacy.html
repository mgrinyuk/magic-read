2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect your email address, password (encrypted/authenticated through third-party providers), and account preferences.

User Content

We may store texts uploaded or pasted by users, saved vocabulary, flashcards, pronunciation practice results, and reading progress.

Technical Information

We may automatically collect device type, browser type, operating system, IP address, language settings, usage analytics, and error logs.

Audio / Speech Data

When using pronunciation or speech recognition features, temporary audio processing may occur through third-party speech recognition providers. We do not intentionally store raw voice recordings unless explicitly stated.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to provide the Magic Read service, process texts and generate learning content, improve pronunciation and reading features, authenticate users, save user progress, provide customer support, improve platform performance and security, and prevent abuse or fraud.

4. Third-Party Services

Magic Read may use third-party providers including Supabase (authentication and database), Stripe (payments and subscriptions), Vercel (hosting), Render (backend hosting), Google Cloud services, speech recognition providers, and analytics providers. These services may process limited user data as required for functionality. We are not responsible for third-party privacy practices.

5. Payments

Payments are processed securely through Stripe. Magic Read does not store full payment card information. Subscription billing, taxes, refunds, and payment processing are handled by Stripe and related financial providers.

6. Cookies

Magic Read may use cookies and similar technologies to keep users logged in, remember preferences, analyze traffic and usage, and improve functionality. Users may disable cookies through browser settings, though some features may stop functioning correctly. See our Cookie Policy for details.

7. Data Retention

We retain user data only as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and improve the platform. Users may request deletion of their account and associated data.

8. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access your data, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, object to certain processing, and request export of your data. Requests may be sent to support@magicread.app.

9. Children

Magic Read is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their passwords.

11. International Users

By using Magic Read, users understand that information may be processed in multiple countries depending on infrastructure and service providers.
