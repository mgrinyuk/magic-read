Delete Your Magic Read Account

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Delete in the app

Open Magic Read, sign in, go to Account, and choose Delete account. The app will ask you to confirm before deleting your account.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email support@magicread.app from the email address connected to your Magic Read account and ask us to delete it.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes your Magic Read account and associated learning data, including saved texts, vocabulary, flashcards, practice progress, usage history, and profile preferences.

What may be retained

We may retain limited records when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, tax, or payment compliance reasons. These records are kept only as long as necessary for those purposes.

Subscriptions

Deleting your Magic Read account does not automatically cancel a subscription managed by Stripe or Google Play. If you have an active subscription, cancel it separately through the payment provider where you purchased it.
