Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings or by emailing support@magicread.app. After cancellation your access continues until the end of the current billing period. You will not be charged again after that.

2. Refunds

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on new subscriptions. If you are not satisfied, contact us within 14 days of your first payment and we will issue a full refund — no questions asked.

After the 14-day period, payments are non-refundable except where required by applicable law (including EU consumer rights legislation).

3. Partial periods

We do not provide prorated refunds for unused days within a billing period after the 14-day guarantee window has passed.

4. Exceptions

If the service was unavailable for an extended period due to a fault on our side, we will assess refund requests on a case-by-case basis.

5. EU / EEA consumers

If you are a consumer in the EU or EEA, you have a statutory right of withdrawal of 14 days from the date of purchase for digital services, unless you have already begun using the service and explicitly waived your right of withdrawal. By starting to use Magic Read immediately after purchase you acknowledge and agree that the right of withdrawal no longer applies, in accordance with Article 16(m) of the Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU. Our voluntary 14-day money-back guarantee is provided in addition to, and does not replace, your statutory rights.

6. How to request a refund

Email support@magicread.app with:

  • Your account email address
  • Date of purchase
  • Reason for your request (optional but helpful)

We will process eligible refunds within 5–10 business days via your original payment method.
